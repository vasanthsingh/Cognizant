Community Bulletin

The community center has announced a weekend clean-up drive aimed at restoring the park and nearby walking paths. Volunteers will be provided with gloves, bags, and safety guidance at the main entrance before the event begins.

Local residents are encouraged to join the food donation campaign, which will collect rice, grains, and packaged essentials for families in need. Donations can be dropped off throughout the week during center hours.

A free health awareness session will also be held on Friday evening, covering hydration, nutrition, and preventive care tips. Health workers from the district clinic will be present to answer questions.

The bulletin board will continue to be updated with upcoming workshops, cultural programs, and volunteer opportunities so that everyone can stay informed and involved in neighborhood activities.
